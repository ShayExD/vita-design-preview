Kitchen cabinets, built to the room

  1. Home/
  2. Products/
  3. Kitchen Cabinets

A kitchen is the one room where storage, circulation and sightlines all have to resolve at once. We design cabinetry around how the room is actually used, then build it to the dimensions of the space rather than to a catalogue module. That means full-height runs where the ceiling allows, drawer banks sized to what goes in them, and appliance surrounds that sit flush instead of almost flush.

Common questions

How long does a custom kitchen take?
From the first consultation to installation, most kitchens run eight to twelve weeks. Design and revisions take two to three weeks of that, fabrication six to eight, and installation is usually under a week for a standard layout.
Can you match cabinetry to an existing room?
Yes. We take finish samples from the existing space and match stain, sheen and profile as closely as the material allows. Wood is a natural material, so an exact match on an aged finish is rarely possible, but we will show you the sample before committing.

Start with a conversation about the room.

Bring measurements if you have them, photographs if you do not. The first consultation is free and there is no obligation to proceed.
