Wood Finishes

  1. Home/
  2. Cabinet Collections/
  3. Wood Finishes

Stained and clear finishes that leave the grain visible. Cherry, walnut and oak each move differently with light, and each ages differently: cherry darkens noticeably in the first year, walnut lightens slightly, oak stays close to where it started. We show samples in the actual room before committing, because showroom lighting is misleading about all three.
