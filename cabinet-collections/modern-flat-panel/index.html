Modern Flat Panel

  1. Home/
  2. Cabinet Collections/
  3. Modern Flat Panel

A flat door with no applied moulding and a minimal reveal. It is the quietest option we build, and the least forgiving: with no profile to break up the surface, everything depends on the flatness of the panel, the consistency of the finish and the evenness of the gaps. Well executed, a flat-panel run reads as a single wall rather than a row of doors.
