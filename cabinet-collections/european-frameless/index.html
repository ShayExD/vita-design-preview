European Frameless

  1. Home/
  2. Cabinet Collections/
  3. European Frameless

Frameless construction removes the face frame entirely, so the door covers the full front of the box. The practical gain is access: drawers run the full internal width, and there is no frame edge to reach past. The visual gain is the tight, consistent reveal across a long run, which is difficult to achieve any other way.
